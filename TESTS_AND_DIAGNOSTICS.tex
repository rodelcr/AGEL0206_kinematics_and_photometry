\hypertarget{a.-foundational-data-and-geometry}{%
\subsubsection{A. Foundational data and geometry}\label{a.-foundational-data-and-geometry}}

\textbf{A1 KCWI cube provenance} --- The cube \texttt{Nov17\_2025\_DESJ0206\_RL\_combined\_icubes\_wcs.fits} is the final multi-night reduction (Aug 29 K409 + Sept 29 K409 + Dec 29 + Nov 17 2025). The
header is mislabeled (DATE-OBS/PROGNAME/PROGPI describe only the last stacking pass). Settled in \texttt{NOTES\_methodology\_2026-04-27.md}. \textbf{Cite the multi-night provenance, not the header}.

\textbf{A2 Systemic redshift} --- Independent line-by-line Gaussian fitting of Ca H+K, {[}OII{]}, G-band, etc. gives z\_systemic = 0.67564 (NIST air wavelengths). DR2 catalog z=0.67511 (used by older
notebooks 03/03b) is offset by \textasciitilde95 km/s from the line-fit value; nb09 uses 0.67564 throughout. Notebook: \texttt{notebooks/04\_redshift\_verification.ipynb} Module:
\texttt{scripts/redshift\_verify.py} (provides ABSORPTION\_LINES / EMISSION\_LINES dicts, \texttt{gaussian\_fit}, \texttt{multi\_line\_fit}, \texttt{oii\_doublet\_fit})

**A3 Effective radius R\_e = 2.305``** --- Mean of F140W and F200LP masked curve-of-growth values (2.168 and 2.441''). The masked CoG zeros mask-flagged HST pixels before the radial integral so the
arc/companion contributions don't bias the bright-end half-light point. Computed in \texttt{scripts/measure\_Re.py} and re-derived in \texttt{scripts/final\_sigma\_e.py:curve\_of\_growth} for the nb09
pipeline. Cross-check: IFU white-light proper-masked CoG gave 2.61" earlier --- the HST-based 2.305" is now the headline.

\textbf{A4 HST-mean center} --- \texttt{photutils.centroid\_2dg} on F140W and F200LP cutouts (with their own \texttt{\_mask.fits} as the mask) gives sub-pixel centroids in each HST WCS. We average in
world coordinates, then propagate to IFU sub-pixel: \texttt{scripts/final\_sigma\_e.py:find\_center}. F140W vs F200LP centroid offset = 0.36" --- flags the F200LP arc-affected detection but the
HST-mean is robust (verified by the 5-center sweep, F1).

\textbf{A5 Stellar mass} --- Bagpipes SED fitting on HST + JWST aperture photometry → log M★/M☉ = 11.33 +0.07/−0.09 (notebook 02). Cross-checked at the Sersic-total level in
\texttt{notebooks/08\_sersic\_total\_photometry.ipynb}.

\hypertarget{b.-pipeline-correctness-audits-scriptsaudit_ppxf_methodology.py}{%
\subsubsection{\texorpdfstring{B. Pipeline correctness audits
(\texttt{scripts/audit\_ppxf\_methodology.py})}{B. Pipeline correctness audits (scripts/audit\_ppxf\_methodology.py)}}\label{b.-pipeline-correctness-audits-scriptsaudit_ppxf_methodology.py}}

Four orthogonal correctness audits run end-to-end on the headline aperture spectrum to verify ppxf usage matches Cappellari (2017, 2023). Saved in \texttt{results/ppxf\_methodology\_audit.npz}.
Documented in \texttt{NOTES\_nb09\_frame\_fix\_and\_final\_sigma\_e\_2026-04-28.md} ADDENDUM 2026-04-29.

\textbf{B1 Instrumental LSF} --- KCWI header DISPSCAL = 0.294 (instrumental σ in pixels) × CD3\_3 = 1.000 Å/pix → FWHM\_inst = 2.355 × 0.294 = 0.692 Å (constant in observed Å). At the rest-frame fit
window (3879--4476 Å), σ\_v\_inst ≈ 12--14 km/s. Galaxy σ ≈ 270 km/s is resolved by \textasciitilde20×. Diagnosed in \texttt{scripts/ifu\_spectral\_resolution.py}.

\textbf{B2 σ\_inst sensitivity} --- Sweep DISPSCAL × \{0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 2.0\}. Max \textbar Δσ\textbar{} = 0.83 km/s across all SPS. Reason: ppxf's \texttt{sps\_lib} clips fwhm\_diff² =
(FWHM\_gal² − FWHM\_tem²).clip(0) (sps\_util.py:169). When templates are intrinsically broader (FSPS, EMILES at our band), no convolution is applied --- σ becomes insensitive to DISPSCAL. LSF is
rock-solid. Script: \texttt{scripts/sigma\_inst\_sensitivity.py}

\textbf{B3 SPS template frames} --- Located the Ca K minimum in each shipped template: - FSPS spectra\_fsps\_9.0.npz: Ca K at 3934.86 Å → +1.20 Å vs air rest = vacuum - EMILES
spectra\_emiles\_9.0.npz: 3933.82 Å → +0.16 Å = air - XSL spectra\_xsl\_9.0.npz: V\_sys closes at +9 km/s with air-conversion → air End-to-end V\_sys closure (NOTES Test 3) confirms.

\textbf{B4 V\_sys air vs vacuum × 5 polynomial degrees} --- Run ppxf at degrees \{15, 18, 21, 24, 27\} for each SPS in both frames. ΔV(air − vac) ≈ +83 km/s for FSPS, ≈ −82 km/s for EMILES \& XSL,
consistent within ±2 km/s across degrees. Frame identification is robust.

\textbf{B5 z × air-vac differential} --- Apply \texttt{vac\_to\_air} at observed wavelengths (KCWI 6500--7500 Å) → v\_offset = −82.7 km/s. Apply at rest wavelengths (3879--4476 Å) → −84.5 km/s.
Differential = 1.83 km/s --- sub-budget. Either convention is defensible; we follow Cappellari's pattern (apply at obs).

\textbf{B6 fwhm\_gal\_dict frame check} --- Confirmed \texttt{\_prep\_spectrum\_for\_ppxf} builds the dict as \texttt{\{"lam":\ lam\_gal\_rest,\ "fwhm":\ fwhm\_gal\_rest\}} in REST frame, matching
\texttt{ppxf\_example\_high\_redshift.py:99-101}. FWHM\_obs = 0.692 Å → FWHM\_rest = 0.413 Å at z=0.67564. \texttt{sps\_util.py:167} interpolates this onto template \texttt{lam\_temp} (also rest),
then \texttt{varsmooth} pre-broadens.

\subsubsection{C. SPS templates and frame-fix}\label{c.-sps-templates-and-frame-fix}}

\textbf{C1 Frame-aware ppxf} --- \texttt{scripts/bootstrap\_ppxf.py} exposes \texttt{frame\_galaxy=\textquotesingle{}auto\textquotesingle{}} which reads
\texttt{SPS\_NATIVE\_FRAME\ =\ \{fsps:\ vacuum,\ emiles:\ air,\ xsl:\ air\}}. The galaxy spectrum is prepared in the matching frame (\texttt{util.vac\_to\_air()} applied as scalar median when needed,
matching \texttt{ppxf\_example\_kinematics\_sdss.py} :127-134). Diagnosed Apr 2026 --- pre-fix the pipeline always converted to air, producing a −90 km/s FSPS V\_sys offset.

\textbf{C2 V\_sys closure} --- Same galaxy spectrum, swap only the frame. Pre-fix: FSPS −90, EMILES +3, XSL +9. Post-fix (frame-aware): FSPS −7, EMILES +3, XSL +9. Split-track collapsed from
\textasciitilde110 → \textasciitilde15 km/s. NOTES Test 3.

\textbf{C3 σ shift from frame fix} --- Side-effect on σ: ≤5 km/s per SPS, ≤2 km/s on the 3-SPS pool. Carried as ±5 km/s in the error budget (ironically dominated by the per-SPS shifts that pool out,
but conservative).

\textbf{C4 3-SPS pooled posterior} --- \texttt{scripts/final\_sigma\_e.py:pool\_sps} concatenates per-SPS bootstrap σ samples after subtracting per-SPS V\_sys medians (so V offsets don't inflate the V
posterior; σ posteriors are concatenated raw --- no V\_sys correction needed for σ). The headline σ\_e(\textless R\_e) is the 16/50/84 percentile of this combined-SPS pool.

\hypertarget{d.-aperture-and-ir-framework}{%
\subsubsection{D. Aperture and I(r) framework}\label{d.-aperture-and-ir-framework}}

\textbf{D1 §6cum cumulative I-weighted ppxf} --- For each spaxel inside R\textless R\_max, build I-weight from the IFU 6500--7500 Å white-light band, drop arc-mask-flagged spaxels, normalize and sum
to a single 1-D spectrum, fit ppxf. This is the headline path --- matches KH13 / Greene+20 / SAURON / ATLAS3D / MaNGA σ\_e definition (Cappellari+2006 eq. 1). Cross-references in
\texttt{\textasciitilde{}/.claude/.../reference\_cumulative\_vs\_annular\_sigma\_e.md}. Code: \texttt{scripts/final\_sigma\_e.py:run\_aperture\_sps}.

\textbf{D2 §6cum vs §7 (annular)} --- §7 discrete Gültekin annular sum at R\textless R\_safe gives 254.99 km/s; §7b flat-σ extrapolation gives 271 km/s; both within 1σ of §6cum's 267.82 km/s. §6cum is
the headline because (a) it matches the literature definition, (b) it preserves LOSVD line-shape information that §7's moment-pooling discards, (c) bright-center I-weighting auto-suppresses arc
contamination.

\textbf{D3 I(r) shape sweep (10 shapes)} --- Hold the spaxel selection fixed (F200-raw mask, R\textless R\_e), vary only the I-weight map. Shapes: 1. IFU\_band --- 6500-7500 Å (headline) 2. IFU\_wl
--- full IFU white-light 3. F140W\_raw --- HST F140W reprojected 4. F200LP\_raw --- HST F200LP reprojected 5. F140W\_arcmasked --- F140W with arc zeroed 6. F200LP\_arcmasked --- F200LP with own
arc-mask zeroed 7. F140W\_1Dcog --- annular-mean F140W → interp1d to spaxels 8. F200LP\_1Dcog --- annular-mean F200LP → interp1d 9. F140W\_Sersic2D --- full 2D Sersic fit 10. F200LP\_Sersic2D --- full
2D Sersic fit (poor fit, n=0.30 --- outlier)

Range excluding F200LP\_Sersic2D outlier = 12.9 km/s → ±13 km/s budget. Script: \texttt{scripts/run\_isource\_shape\_sweep.py} Analysis: \texttt{scripts/analyze\_isource\_shape\_sweep.py} Caches:
\texttt{results/annular\_bootstrap\_07c\_ishape/\{shape\}\_\{sps\}.npz} Figure: \texttt{results/figures/nb07c\_ishape\_sweep.png}

\textbf{D4 I-shape sweep refresh post-frame-fix} --- Original sweep at N=250 predated the frame fix. Re-ran at N=500 with \texttt{frame\_galaxy=\textquotesingle{}auto\textquotesingle{}} on 2026-04-29.
Per-SPS σ shifts \textless0.5 km/s; spread unchanged at 12.9 km/s; ±13 budget validated. Pre-frame caches preserved at \texttt{results/annular\_bootstrap\_07c\_ishape\_oldframe/}. NOTES addendum (d).

\textbf{D5 Aperture choice} --- Three apertures considered: R\textless R\_e/8 (=0.288``), R\textless R\_e/2 (=1.152''), R\textless R\_e (=2.305``). R\textless R\_e/8 dropped because it sits inside
half the seeing FWHM (=0.64'') --- only 3 spaxels, and the inner σ would underestimate due to seeing-broadened sampling. R\textless R\_e is the paper headline; R\textless R\_e/2 is quoted as a
gradient diagnostic.

\textbf{D6 Equal-N vs equal-width annular binning} --- For the §7 cross-check only. Equal-N inside R\_safe = 3R\_e/4 + 1 outer flagged bin (\texttt{notebooks/07c}) gives balanced bootstrap variance
per bin and isolates arc contamination in the outer bin. Equal-width annuli (\texttt{notebooks/07b}, 5 bins) shifted σ\_e by \textasciitilde10 km/s --- at the SPS-systematic level (±24). Equal-N is
the §7 paper choice.

\hypertarget{e.-mask-treatment}{%
\subsubsection{E. Mask treatment}\label{e.-mask-treatment}}

\textbf{E1 F200LP arc mask reprojection} --- F200LP \texttt{\_mask.fits} (HST 0.04``/pix, arc-tuned) is reprojected to the IFU 0.30''/spax grid via \texttt{scipy.ndimage.map\_coordinates(order=0)}
(nearest- neighbor). 69 of 10000 spaxels (0.69\%) flagged. Inside R\textless R\_e: 38 of 184 spaxels in the aperture, \textasciitilde21\% --- but only \textasciitilde27\% of the I-weight (arc spaxels
are mostly off-center).

\textbf{E2 Hard-mask (w=0.0) headline} --- track A. \textbf{E3 No-mask (w=1.0) sensitivity} --- track B. Δ\_mask = −15.0 km/s. \textbf{E4 Soft-mask single point (w=0.5)} --- track C. Δ\_soft = −9.3
km/s. \textbf{E5 5-point mask-weight sweep (w∈\{0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1\})} --- Per-step σ drops at R\textless R\_e: 5.0 → 4.3 → 3.5 → 2.2 km/s. Quadratic c = +7.3 (concave-up, super-linear).
Threshold-dominated bias: a few heavily-arc-contaminated spaxels carry most of the bias. First 25\% of arc weight captures 34\% of the total mask sensitivity. Scripts:
\texttt{scripts/soft\_mask\_track.py}, \texttt{scripts/analyze\_mask\_weight\_sweep.py} Figure: \texttt{results/figures/nb09\_mask\_weight\_sweep.png} NOTES addendums (b) and (c).

\textbf{E6 Mask dilation} --- Tested whether expanding the F200 mask further reduces σ. \textbf{Negative finding}: dilation inflates σ via noise --- the dropped spaxels are S/N contributors, not bias
contributors. Memory: \texttt{\textasciitilde{}/.claude/.../project\_nb07d\_overmasking\_finding.md}. Notebook: \texttt{nb07d}.

\textbf{E7 Arc-spectrum subtraction sibling} --- Build an arc-only spectrum from F200-masked spaxels, fit α × arc + β × deflector model, subtract. Result matches §6cum within 0.1 km/s →
\emph{residual} arc dilution is sub-dominant at production statistics. The F200 mask already does the heavy lifting. Notebook: \texttt{nb07e}.

\subsubsection{F. Centering}\label{f.-centering}}

\textbf{F1 5-center sweep} --- Tested HST\_mean (paper choice), F140W only, F200LP only, IFU peak, arc-suppressed IFU peak. σ\_e spread = 3.7 km/s. Carried as ±4 km/s in the error budget. Notes:

\hypertarget{g.-bootstrap-and-polynomial-degree}{%
\subsubsection{G. Bootstrap and polynomial degree}\label{g.-bootstrap-and-polynomial-degree}}

\textbf{G1 Wild bootstrap} --- Hybrid Rademacher sign-flip × local-residual scaling (75-pix rolling window). For each iteration: residual = (galaxy − bestfit) × sign, scaled by local σ. Re-fit, record
V/σ. Implemented in \texttt{scripts/bootstrap\_ppxf.py:run\_bootstrap\_single\_degree}.

\textbf{G2 Parallel bootstrap} --- joblib + threadpoolctl pinning BLAS=1 per worker (otherwise BLAS thread oversubscription pathology --- saw 4-hour runtimes). 8 workers gives \textasciitilde6 min for
6 fits at N=500. Script: \texttt{scripts/bootstrap\_ppxf\_parallel.py:run\_bootstrap\_single\_degree\_parallel}.

\textbf{G3 Polynomial degree sweep} --- Degrees 15-29 (15 values) per fit. Diagnostic: σ vs degree should be flat within bootstrap envelope; monotonic trend indicates the polynomial is absorbing LOSVD
signal. Plotted at \texttt{nb09\ §6.5} and saved to \texttt{results/figures/nb09\_sigma\_vs\_degree.png}. Saturation confirmed at this degree range --- picked from earlier \texttt{notebooks/03c}
analysis.

\textbf{G4 N=50 vs N=500} --- N=50 smoke and N=500 production agree on σ\_e to within 1 km/s. N=50 is \textasciitilde6 min, N=500 is \textasciitilde50 min. Production headline is N=500; the smoke is
for quick rebuilds.
