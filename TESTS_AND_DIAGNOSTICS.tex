\section{Tests \& Diagnostics --- AGEL0206 σ\_e ApJL paper}\label{tests-diagnostics-agel0206-ux3c3_e-apjl-paper}}

\textbf{Last updated:} 2026-05-27 (M11 rigorous re-derivation of carried systematics)

\textbf{Headline (NEW \texttt{\_mtwdo\_} + bad-pixel mask + Balmer-unmasked + He I 3819 mask + M10 full sky-line audit + M11 cube-matched systematic re-derivation, wide arc-masked window):}
σ\_e(\textless R\_e) = \textbf{270 ± 12 km/s} = 269.62 ± 11.77 km/s (stat ±5 ⊕ I-shape ±2.27 ⊕ mask ±6.65 ⊕ frame ±5 ⊕ centering ±4 ⊕ window ±3.82 \textbf{⊕ reduction ±3.45}) Asymmetric form: 269.62
−11.98 / +11.57 km/s. Source: \texttt{scripts/run\_wide\_sigma\_e.py\ -\/-cube\ new\_clean\_hei}. M11 sweep caches: \texttt{results/\{ishape\_sweep,maskweight\_sweep\}\_wR3800\_5400\_arcmask\_M10/} +
\texttt{results/nb09a\_wavelength\_sweep\_M10/}. \textbf{The carried ±15 fit-window component dropped to ±3.82 on the cleaned NEW cube} --- the dominant source of the ±6 km/s total-budget tightening.

\textbf{Second-reduction cross-check (OLD cube + bad-pixel mask + Balmer-unmasked + He I + M10 sky masks):} σ\_e(\textless R\_e) = 262.72 ± 17.99 km/s (asymmetric −18.10 / +17.88). The 6.90 km/s Δ
between the two CLEANED + He-I + M10-sky-masked reductions sets the refined ±3.45 km/s reduction-pass systematic (was ±3.86 from clean+He-I-only cubes; M10 sky masks tighten the inter-reduction gap
further).

\textbf{Narrow-window cross-check (Ca H+K + G, OLD cube):} σ\_e(\textless R\_e) = \textbf{268 ± 30 km/s} = 267.95 ± 30.10 km/s (stat ±24 ⊕ I-shape ±3.7 ⊕ mask ±7.5 ⊕ frame ±5 ⊕ centering ±4 ⊕ window
±15)

\textbf{Three-way consistency:} the headline (271.87, new clean + He I) and narrow-window cross-check (267.95, old narrow) are within 4 km/s --- both inside each other's 1σ stat error. The pre-He-I
new-cube wide-window value (268.98) was within 1 km/s of the narrow window; adding the He I mask moves the wide headline \textasciitilde3 km/s redder of the narrow value, but still well within
consistency.

\textbf{Method (wide, headline):} Single ppxf fit on the I-weighted aperture spectrum at R\textless R\_e=2.305" over rest 3800--5400 Å with explicit z=1.302 source-emission masks (MgII λ2800,
{[}OII{]} λ3727, \textbf{He I 3819} (M8), {[}NeIII{]}/Mg b cluster) + bad-pixel mask + Balmer kept in fit, F200LP spatial arc mask, frame-aware (per-SPS vac/air), SPS-pooled (FSPS+EMILES+XSL) wild
bootstrap × polynomial-degree posterior.

\textbf{Source-emission verification figure (appendix candidate):} integrated arc spectrum at \texttt{AGEL\_0206\_ApJL\_Figures/AGEL0206\_arc\_source\_spectrum.pdf} --- direct extraction from the 69
arc-flagged spaxels (deflector continuum subtracted) showing {[}OII{]} 3727 + H9 + {[}NeIII{]} + He I 3819 in source-rest frame and the same lines mapped to def-rest (the headline fit window).

\textbf{Method (narrow, cross-check):} Same machinery but on obs-frame 6500--7500 Å (rest 3879--4476 Å, anchored on Ca H+K and G-band only) --- the §6cum cumulative I-weighted ppxf path retained from
nb07c.

\item

\hypertarget{j.-wide-arc-masked-window-current-paper-headline-may-2026}{%
\subsubsection{J. Wide arc-masked window (current paper headline, May 2026)}\label{j.-wide-arc-masked-window-current-paper-headline-may-2026}}

This section documents the May 8--13 work that elevated the rest 3800--5400 Å arc-masked window above the narrow Ca H+K + G-band window as the paper headline. The wide window had been off-limits in
the nb07c era because of source-emission contamination from the z=1.302 spiral and template issues --- both are now resolved.

\textbf{J1 Wavelength-window sweep (nb09a)} --- \texttt{notebooks/09a\_wavelength\_window\_sweep.ipynb} + \texttt{scripts/run\_window\_sweep.py}. Bootstrap σ\_e at 15 candidate windows from def-rest
3500--5500 down to narrow Ca H+K only. Result: wR3800\_5400 wins on statistical precision (\textasciitilde4× more spectral pixels than narrow). Blue edge set by \textbf{XSL template floor at def-rest
3675 Å with 5\% velocity padding} (J2 below); red edge set by KCWI red-channel coverage at z=0.67564.

\textbf{Provenance of the 3800--5400 range:} NOT from a specific literature prescription --- it's set by the data (XSL template floor + retaining Ca H+K to cross-anchor with the narrow w6500\_7500
result). Closest literature precedent for going as blue as 3800 Å is \textbf{LEGA-C (Bezanson+2018, van der Wel+2021)} at z\textasciitilde0.7 using \textasciitilde3500--5500 rest. SAURON/ATLAS3D's
canonical kinematic window is much narrower (4800--5380; Mg b + Fe5270 + Fe5335 only). The Lick-tradition ``4000--5400 (no Ca H+K)'' range is captured by the \texttt{wR4000\_5400\_arcmask} sensitivity
check (orthogonal Hβ + Mg b feature set) --- NOT the headline.

\textbf{J2 Source-emission contamination discovery (nb09a §3a, nb09b §8)} --- Wide-window ppxf wild-bootstrap posterior was bimodal (σ ≈ 250 alongside σ ≈ 100--150; frac\textless200 km/s = 60--80\%)
at deg ≥ 23. Caused by source-frame emission lines from the z=1.302 spiral arc contaminating the deflector spectrum. Empirically identified as \textgreater4σ excess above local continuum:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{def-rest 3835--3855 Å} = Mg II λλ2796/2803 doublet from z=1.302
\item
  \textbf{def-rest 4525--4545 Å} = \textbf{O₂ A-band telluric leading-edge residual at obs 7593--7626 Å} (NOT source emission --- originally flagged as a \textasciitilde7.9σ wild-bootstrap excess and
  provisionally tagged ``source rest 3300 Å feature''; relabeled 2026-05-18 after the spike was confirmed present at the same wavelength in deflector R\textless1.5″, the CLAUDE.md sky box, and a 4--8″
  off-deflector annulus, with deflector/off-source amplitude ratio 1.11× despite a 17.5× continuum-brightness ratio. See \texttt{NOTES\_4534A\_spike\_investigation\_2026-05-18.md} and
  \texttt{results/figures/spike\_4534A\_diagnostic.png}.)
\item
  \textbf{def-rest 5115--5135 Å} = source {[}O II{]} λλ3727/3729 --- sits \textbf{on the Mg b absorption}; was distorting the FSPS Mg b depth ratio to 1.18
\item
  \textbf{def-rest 5260--5340 Å} = Mg b / {[}Ne III{]} λ3869 cluster from source
\end{itemize}

Total: 212 of 2374 pixels (8.9\% of fit window) masked.

\textbf{J5 Sersic2D bound-fix (\texttt{scripts/run\_isource\_shape\_sweep.py}, 2026-05-11)} --- The I-shape sweep was inflated by a non-physical Sersic2D fit. Original script allowed
\texttt{n\ ∈\ {[}0.3,\ 8.0{]}} and \texttt{ellip\ ∈\ {[}0.0,\ 0.95{]}}. The F200LP fit escaped to \textbf{n=0.30, ellip=0.00} --- a degenerate flat-disk minimum (astropy flagged it as unsuccessful).
The flat profile under-weighted the center and yielded σ = 271.8 km/s, a +20 km/s outlier inflating the I-shape std to ±5.4. Fix:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Tighten bounds: \texttt{n\ ∈\ {[}1.0,\ 6.0{]}}, \texttt{ellip\ ∈\ {[}0.0,\ 0.6{]}}, \texttt{r\_eff\ ∈\ {[}r\_eff\_pix\ ×\ 0.5,\ r\_eff\_pix\ ×\ 2.0{]}} (was ×0.3--×3.0)
\item
  Try 3 initial-condition grids: (n\_init, ellip\_init) ∈ \{(1.5, 0.05), (2.0, 0.2), (3.5, 0.2)\}; keep lowest-χ² fit
\item
  F200LP now converges to a physical n that gives σ = 251.4 km/s
\item
  F140W was already converging at n=2.14, fix did not change it materially
\end{itemize}

After the fix, I-shape std collapsed: \textbf{±5.4 → ±1.5 km/s} at wide arc-masked. Universal fix applied to the I-shape sweep code path --- all downstream sweeps now use these bounds.

\hypertarget{whats-intentionally-not-done}{%
\subsection{5. What's intentionally NOT done}\label{whats-intentionally-not-done}}

\textbf{Updated 2026-05-13} with status changes after the wide arc-masked window work and the Sersic2D bound-fix.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Re-run nb09 at higher N} (e.g.~N=2000): the pooled posterior is already smooth; at the wide arc-masked window the stat error is ±6 km/s while the fit-window systematic dominates at ±15 km/s.
  More N is wasted compute.
\item
  \textbf{\sout{Re-fit Sersic2D F200LP} DONE 2026-05-11} --- the n=0.30 fit was a parameter-bound pathology, not a UV-arc artifact. Bound-fix (J7) tightened bounds to n ∈ {[}1.0, 6.0{]} with
  multi-init grid. F200LP\_Sersic2D now converges to a physical fit (σ=251.4 km/s) and is \textbf{kept in} the I-shape budget, not flagged as an outlier.
\item
  \textbf{Voronoi binning} --- Test 18 / nb05x --- abandoned; not pursued at N=500.
\item
  \textbf{\sout{PowerBin spatial binning} PARTIAL} --- Test 19 narrow-window rejected was correct at the time. \textbf{At the wide arc-masked window (K2 / nb11) PowerBin now works} for the inner
  \textasciitilde5 of 7 bins; 2 outer bins still hit σ \textgreater{} 400 (irreducible KCWI per-spaxel S/N at aperture edge). Not used as headline binning scheme but published as supplementary σ map.
\item
  \textbf{\sout{Per-spaxel rotation map at production statistics} DONE (K1 / nb11)} --- at the wide arc-masked window, S/N ≥ 5 gives 17 central spaxels with clean σ values and a visible σ(R) gradient.
  No coherent rotation detected above the noise (consistent with elliptical-bulge expectation). Published as supplementary kinematic map.
\item
  \textbf{Full nb07a Sersic-I revisit at the post-frame-fix headline} --- superseded by the I-shape sweep (D3/D4/J6) which covers the Sersic2D shape with the bound-fix applied.
\item
  \textbf{Re-quote σ\_e on KH13/Bell+2003 stellar masses for the M\_BH--M★ plot} --- Figure 3 keeps the KH13 dual-M/L dynamical bulge masses for the local comparison sample. The Greene+2020 list match
  (L3) restricts to the same 60 galaxies as Greene+2020 Fig 10 but with our (better) bulge mass values.
\item
  \textbf{Cross-match nb09a ``narrow'' window to other AGEL targets} --- paper is single-target. The arc-emission-mask methodology (J2/J3) is reusable for future AGEL targets where source z is known,
  but applying it elsewhere is not in scope for the ApJL.
\end{itemize}
